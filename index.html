Why this exists MITRE ATT&CK is an index of what adversaries already do to us. The techniques are fully known; what is missing is the victim's field manual. Each GHOSTLIGHT file picks one technique and answers two questions in order: how do I know if this exact thing was used against me, and what exactly do I do about it. Attack first, so you respect it.
